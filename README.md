## Selected work

### [KODA / TRF Risk Case Study](https://github.com/IanHu0508/koda-trf-risk-case-study)

Reproducible Monte Carlo scenario analysis of asymmetric FX payoff and tail risk, with deterministic artifacts, sensitivity analysis, tests, and CI. The model is a stress study rather than a reconstructed contract or risk-neutral pricing claim.

### [Company Research Automation](https://github.com/IanHu0508/company-research-automation)

Evidence-controlled listed-company research workflow with a runnable bear/base/bull SOTP valuation slice, explicit cutoffs, reproducible decimal arithmetic, and machine-verifiable result packets.
